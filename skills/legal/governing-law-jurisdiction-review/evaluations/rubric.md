# Evaluation Rubric — governing-law-jurisdiction-review

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Evidence grounding (dominant):** clauses classified from their actual
  wording (exclusive vs non-exclusive); scope and exclusions read precisely.
  An exclusivity misread caps this at 2.
- **Risk identification:** CISG traps, asymmetries, law/forum incoherence,
  enforcement-reach gaps surfaced.
- **Instruction adherence:** no predictions of judicial treatment; mandatory-
  law questions flagged not adjudicated.
- **Usefulness:** burden analysis grounded in the parties' real locations
  and assets.

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Misclassifies a non-exclusive submission clause as exclusive (or vice
  versa).
- Misses an unexcluded-CISG issue in a cross-border goods scenario built to
  test it.
- Predicts that a court will or won't honor the clause, or which mandatory
  laws override.
- Ignores a stated asymmetry (one party may sue anywhere, the other is
  locked to one forum).

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 75% of weighted maximum.
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Instruction adherence ≥ 4; Evidence
  grounding ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
