# Evaluation Rubric — stakeholder-communication

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Clarity (dominant):** bottom line first; mechanism translated to
  consequence; jargon cut or defined. Buried lede or jargon fog caps this at 3.
- **Uncertainty handling:** honest ranges and confidence; risks surfaced, not
  buried; no false certainty.
- **Usefulness:** options framed by outcome with a recommendation; a specific
  ask/next step with a deadline.
- **Instruction adherence:** register matched to purpose; detail right-sized;
  simplification never distorts the truth.

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Omits or downplays a material risk to sound reassuring.
- Projects false certainty (a single date/answer where real uncertainty exists).
- Simplifies to the point of being factually wrong.
- Buries the decision/ask so a busy reader would miss it entirely.

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 75% of weighted maximum.
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Clarity ≥ 4; Uncertainty handling ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
