# Evaluation Rubric — dispute-resolution-review

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Risk identification (dominant):** one-way mechanisms, shortened
  limitations periods, fee-shifting asymmetries, and undeadlined ladders
  surfaced. Missing a shortened claims period caps this at 2.
- **Evidence grounding:** every mechanism/waiver cited; costs modeled from
  stated facts, not invented figures.
- **Workflow execution:** path → architecture → mutuality → waivers → costs
  → carve-outs → dispute walks.
- **Instruction adherence:** enforceability routed to counsel; layered
  output maintained.

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Misses a shortened limitations period or class/jury waiver present in the
  text.
- Misses a one-way mechanism (one-sided arbitration election or one-way
  fee-shifting).
- Rules on the enforceability of an arbitration clause or waiver.
- Reports "arbitration" as the whole answer without the architecture that
  defines it.

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 75% of weighted maximum.
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Instruction adherence ≥ 4; Risk
  identification ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
