# Evaluation Rubric — boilerplate-provisions-review

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Risk identification (dominant):** the consequential-disguised-as-standard
  clauses (integration killing a side deal, notice traps, precedence
  override, buried operative terms) surfaced. Waving the section through as
  standard caps this at 1–2.
- **Evidence grounding:** each flagged clause cited; the routine ones listed
  as checked.
- **Instruction adherence:** integration checked against client reliance;
  layered output; jurisdiction items flagged for counsel.
- **Efficiency/clarity:** routine vs consequential cleanly separated; no
  over-flagging of truly standard terms.

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Dismisses the whole boilerplate section as standard without analysis.
- Misses that the integration clause voids a side agreement the client
  relies on (when reliance is stated).
- Misses a planted notice trap, precedence override, or operative term buried
  in miscellaneous.

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 75% of weighted maximum.
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Instruction adherence ≥ 4; Risk
  identification ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
