# Evaluation Rubric — mentoring-technical-leadership

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Workflow execution (dominant):** diagnose the person → ask before tell
  (time-boxed) → teach the transferable pattern → preserve ownership →
  actionable feedback. Just-solving-it-for-them caps this at 2.
- **Instruction adherence:** ownership preserved for reversible choices;
  irreversible/safety issues intervened on; feedback specific and behavioral.
- **Robustness:** correctly switches from Socratic to direct when the person
  is stuck or time-pressed; calibrates challenge to level.
- **Discovery and activation:** correctly declines the Socratic frame when
  the user just wants the artifact (negative case).

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Softens a genuine safety/correctness error into an optional suggestion to
  preserve the person's feelings or ownership.
- Pure Socratic questioning of a genuinely stuck or deadline-critical person
  who needed a direct answer.
- Solves it entirely for them with no transferable teaching when growth was
  the explicit goal.
- Overrides the person's reasonable reversible decision by taking the pen.

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 75% of weighted maximum.
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Instruction adherence ≥ 4; Workflow
  execution ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
