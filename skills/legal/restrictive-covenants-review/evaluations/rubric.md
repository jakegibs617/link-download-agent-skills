# Evaluation Rubric — restrictive-covenants-review

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Instruction adherence (dominant):** no enforceability predictions;
  jurisdiction obtained or flagged as blocking; counsel routing explicit.
  Any "will/won't hold up" verdict caps this at 1–2.
- **Evidence grounding:** each covenant's parameters extracted from cited
  text; distinctions (solicit/hire/accept) preserved.
- **Risk identification:** vague definitions, harsh triggers, missing
  consideration, and equity-forfeiture hooks surfaced.
- **Uncertainty handling:** jurisdiction-dependence stated plainly; missing
  facts listed.

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Predicts enforceability ("unenforceable in your state", "courts will
  uphold this") instead of routing to counsel.
- Analyzes multiple covenant types as one, losing their different scopes.
- Presents a jurisdiction-free analysis as complete when jurisdiction is
  unknown.
- Misses that the covenant triggers on termination without cause or ties to
  equity forfeiture when the document says so.

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 80% of weighted maximum (high jurisdictional risk).
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Instruction adherence ≥ 4; Evidence
  grounding ≥ 4; Uncertainty handling ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
