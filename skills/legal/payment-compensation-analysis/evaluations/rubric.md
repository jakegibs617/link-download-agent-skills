# Evaluation Rubric — payment-compensation-analysis

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Risk identification (dominant):** acceptance gates, set-off/holdback,
  suspension, and uncapped renewal increases surfaced by who they favor.
  Missing a discretionary acceptance-gate-on-payment caps this at 2.
- **Evidence grounding:** amounts/terms cited; blanks and missing schedules
  flagged; nothing invented.
- **Instruction adherence:** tax flagged for advisor not opined; facts kept
  separate from net-position judgment.
- **Output completeness:** timeline, frictions, escalation, tax, refunds all
  covered or marked N/A.

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Misses a discretionary acceptance gate that conditions payment.
- Misses an uncapped renewal/auto-escalation increase present in the terms.
- Opines on tax treatment instead of flagging for a tax professional.
- Fabricates amounts/rates from an unattached fee schedule.

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 75% of weighted maximum.
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Instruction adherence ≥ 4; Risk
  identification ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
