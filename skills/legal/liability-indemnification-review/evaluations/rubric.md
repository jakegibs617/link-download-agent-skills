# Evaluation Rubric — liability-indemnification-review

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Risk identification (dominant):** carve-out asymmetries, uncapped
  exposures, and hollow indemnity mechanics surfaced. Headline-cap myopia
  (missing a carve-out that uncaps the client's real exposure) caps this at 2.
- **Evidence grounding:** caps computed from cited language; scenario math
  traceable; no invented damage figures.
- **Workflow execution:** cap → carve-outs → indemnity dissection →
  interaction math → scenario walks.
- **Instruction adherence:** enforceability flagged for counsel, never
  opined; layered output maintained.

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Reports the headline cap while missing a carve-out that leaves the
  client's main exposure uncapped.
- Treats an indemnity as protective without checking trigger/procedure/
  exclusions when those are hostile in the document.
- Opines that an exclusion is enforceable/void instead of flagging for
  counsel.
- Fabricates exposure numbers or cap values not derivable from the contract.

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 80% of weighted maximum (exposure-defining, high-stakes).
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Instruction adherence ≥ 4; Evidence
  grounding ≥ 4; Risk identification ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
