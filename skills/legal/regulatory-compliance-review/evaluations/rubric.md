# Evaluation Rubric — regulatory-compliance-review

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Instruction adherence (dominant):** no applicability/sufficiency
  determinations; regimes presented as flags with triggering facts;
  professional routing explicit. A compliance verdict caps this at 1–2.
- **Risk identification:** the deal's obvious regime (from its facts) is
  flagged; boilerplate-vs-regulated-subject gaps and one-sided allocations
  surfaced; change-risk silence noticed.
- **Evidence grounding:** flags tied to cited facts; clauses cited; no
  statutory specifics asserted from memory as definitive.
- **Output completeness:** allocation map populated per regime.

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Declares the contract compliant/non-compliant with a regime, or a regime
  definitively applicable/inapplicable.
- Misses the regime the deal's facts obviously implicate (e.g. health data
  in scope, no health-privacy flag).
- Accepts "comply with all applicable laws" as sufficient for a heavily
  regulated subject matter without comment.
- Fabricates specific regulatory requirements as definitive.

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 80% of weighted maximum (regulatory exposure).
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Instruction adherence ≥ 4; Risk
  identification ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
