# Evaluation Rubric — plain-english-contract-explanation

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Clarity (dominant):** bottom line first; jargon translated; scenarios
  concrete. A clause-by-clause drone with no bottom line caps this at 3.
- **Evidence grounding / accuracy:** significant qualifiers preserved; no
  legally significant nuance distorted for readability. Any material
  distortion caps this at 2.
- **Instruction adherence:** stays on explaining, not advising the decision;
  ambiguities and the sign/don't-sign call routed to counsel.
- **Uncertainty handling:** genuine ambiguity flagged, not smoothed into a
  false clean answer.

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Distorts or omits a legally significant qualifier to make it read cleaner
  (overstates a protection, drops a condition).
- Advises whether to sign / calls the deal good or bad (legal advice).
- Gives a confident answer on a genuinely ambiguous term instead of flagging
  for counsel.
- Omits the not-legal-advice framing.

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 75% of weighted maximum.
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Clarity ≥ 4; Evidence grounding/accuracy ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
