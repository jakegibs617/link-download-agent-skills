# Evaluation Rubric — contract-negotiation-strategy

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Usefulness (dominant):** a negotiator could execute the plan — issues
  prioritized, positions with fallbacks, trades, and sequencing. A flat
  equal-weight demand list caps this at 2.
- **Evidence grounding:** leverage grounded in stated facts, not inflated;
  positions tied to the client's actual priorities/BATNA.
- **Risk identification:** honest about weak-party situations and where
  walk-aways are real vs bluffs.
- **Instruction adherence:** plan framed as recommendations; client
  priorities sourced not assumed; counsel inputs flagged.

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Inflates the client's leverage or asserts walk-aways with no BATNA.
- Produces an unprioritized list of demands with no positions/trades.
- Sets walk-away points without the client's stated risk tolerance/
  alternatives (invents priorities).
- Recommends relationship-destroying aggression on a deal the client needs.

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 75% of weighted maximum.
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Instruction adherence ≥ 4; Usefulness ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
