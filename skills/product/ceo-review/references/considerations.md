# CEO Review — Full Question List

Questions per focus area. Answer from repo evidence; note explicitly when the
repo is silent. Silence is a gradeable finding, not a gap to work around.

## Vision & product-market fit

- Is the vision specific and falsifiable, or could any product claim it? A vision
  that can't fail isn't a vision.
- Who exactly is the customer? Is there a named *excluded* customer — a sign of
  real positioning?
- What evidence of demand exists — user research, comps, waitlists, community
  signals — versus founder conviction alone?
- What is the differentiation or moat? Would it survive a fast-follower cloning
  the surface features?
- Does the product as scoped actually test the vision, or does it test something
  easier?

*If the vision statement itself is the problem — generic, unfalsifiable, targets
everyone — that is `strong-product-vision`'s object. Grade it here; hand the
rewrite there.*

## Business model & unit economics

**Scoreboard depth only.** These questions establish whether the thinking exists
and survives scrutiny. They do not produce the model. Any answer that requires
building a number — computing a CAC, projecting break-even, sizing runway —
belongs to `cfo`. Grade the hole, name it, hand it over.

- How does this make money? Is the monetization model named and specific —
  pricing, tiers, purchase drivers?
- Are there numbers anywhere — cost to build, cost to run, plausible revenue — or
  only qualitative principles?
- Has anyone estimated what a customer costs to acquire and what they are worth?
  (For F2P games: retention economics, conversion rate, ARPPU, whale dependence.)
  The question is whether an estimate *exists*, not what it should be.
- What revenue is deliberately left on the table — ethical constraints, brand,
  regulation — and is that tradeoff priced in anywhere?
- What is the path from zero to first revenue, and how long is it?

## Market & competition

- How big is the addressable market, and does anything in the repo size it?
- Who are the named competitors, and what happens when they notice?
- Why now — what changed to make this winnable today?
- Platform and channel risk: does distribution depend on a gatekeeper (App Store,
  Play Store, a single marketplace, one API vendor)? What is the policy or
  rating exposure?

## Execution

- What is the delta between plan and reality — code written versus documents
  written?
- Do team, budget, and timeline constraints exist on paper? Without them, no
  scope judgment in the plan is evaluable.
- What do velocity signals show — commit cadence, revision history, staleness of
  docs? Is the work concentrated in one person?
- What is the nearest shippable slice that produces a real-world learning, and is
  anything blocking starting it?
- Is the riskiest bet scheduled first, or is the plan retiring zero risk until
  late?

## Risk

- What single assumption, if wrong, kills the project? Is it being tested or
  deferred?
- Top technical risk, top market risk, top platform or regulatory risk — one
  each, ranked.
- Single points of failure: one person, one vendor, one channel, one feature?
- Content, brand, or legal exposure — theme sensitivity, IP, age ratings, data
  privacy?
- What is the cost of being wrong late versus finding out early, and does the
  plan choose early?
