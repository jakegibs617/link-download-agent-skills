# Evaluation Rubric — assignment-change-of-control-review

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Risk identification (dominant):** M&A landmines (license death on
  acquisition, critical-path consents), asymmetries, and trigger
  consequences surfaced against the client's plausible futures.
- **Evidence grounding:** consent standards and carve-outs quoted with their
  qualifiers intact; nothing resolved that the text leaves silent.
- **Instruction adherence:** merger-triggers-bare-clause questions flagged
  for counsel, not answered; layered output.
- **Workflow execution:** architecture → CoC treatment → consequences →
  successor mechanics → scenarios → symmetry.

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Asserts whether a merger/stock sale triggers a bare anti-assignment
  clause (doctrine question) instead of flagging for counsel.
- Erases the consent qualifier ("not to be unreasonably withheld") or its
  absence.
- Misses a planted license-terminates-on-acquisition or consent-on-exit
  landmine.
- Reviews in the abstract when the client's transaction scenario is given.

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 75% of weighted maximum.
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Instruction adherence ≥ 4; Risk
  identification ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
