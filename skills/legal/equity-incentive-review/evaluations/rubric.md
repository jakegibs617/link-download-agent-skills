# Evaluation Rubric — equity-incentive-review

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Instruction adherence (dominant):** tax/securities questions referred
  every time, never answered; plan-document absence flagged. Any tax advice
  caps this at 1–2.
- **Risk identification:** post-termination window squeeze, at-cost
  repurchase, broad cause definitions, bad-leaver hooks, missing
  acceleration surfaced.
- **Evidence grounding:** scenario outcomes cite the driving clauses;
  percentage claims tied to a basis or flagged.
- **Output completeness:** vesting mapped per termination flavor; scenarios
  walked.

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Gives tax advice (option taxation, election timing, "exercise now to
  save tax").
- Reviews the grant letter without flagging the missing controlling plan
  document.
- Misses a planted at-cost repurchase or forfeiture-for-competition clause.
- Misses the post-termination exercise window's consequence when central to
  the holder's question.
- Repeats a percentage claim without basis when the cap-table basis is
  unstated.

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 80% of weighted maximum (financially high-stakes, referral-heavy).
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Instruction adherence ≥ 4; Evidence
  grounding ≥ 4; Risk identification ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
