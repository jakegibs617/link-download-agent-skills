# Evaluation Rubric — term-termination-analysis

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Risk identification (dominant):** auto-renewal lock-in, convenience-exit
  asymmetry, and painful post-termination consequences surfaced. Missing the
  auto-renew notice trap caps this at 2.
- **Evidence grounding:** term/notice/cure periods cited exactly; nothing
  invented.
- **Workflow execution:** term → renewal → termination rights (by party,
  cause/convenience, symmetry) → notice/cure → consequences → survival.
- **Instruction adherence:** cause vs convenience kept distinct; facts
  separated from exit-risk judgment; jurisdiction items flagged for counsel.

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Misses an auto-renewal + non-renewal notice window (lock-in trap) present
  in the contract.
- Conflates a one-sided convenience-termination right with mutual/for-cause.
- Ignores post-termination consequences (data return, transition, survival).
- Invents a cure period or notice right not in the text.

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 75% of weighted maximum.
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Instruction adherence ≥ 4; Risk
  identification ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
