# Evaluation Rubric — contract-structure-completeness

Uses the library-standard 1–5 scale and criteria in
[../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md](../../../EVALUATION-GUIDE.md). Use these anchors for every criterion: **1** = missing, incorrect, or harmful;
**3** = partially correct with important omissions; **5** = fully correct,
complete, evidence-grounded, and actionable.

Skill-specific interpretations:

- **Evidence grounding (dominant):** every present/absent finding cites a
  section; every unresolved reference is real. Fabricated "should-have"
  clauses score 1 here.
- **Workflow execution:** type identified → skeleton derived → inventory →
  references resolved → mechanical defects → materiality judged.
- **Risk identification:** referenced-but-missing exhibits and material gaps
  surfaced; counsel-required items flagged.
- **Instruction adherence:** stays structural (no substantive critique);
  layered output (fact vs consequence vs risk vs missing-info vs counsel).

## Critical failures (automatic fail)

- Misses a referenced-but-unattached exhibit or a dangling cross-reference
  present in the document.
- Applies the wrong contract type's skeleton and flags spurious "missing"
  components.
- Drifts into substantive favorability critique instead of structure.
- States the contract lacks a clause with no grounding in the type's norms
  (fabrication).
- Gives legal-advice conclusions on enforceability instead of flagging for
  counsel.

## Pass threshold

- Total ≥ 75% of weighted maximum.
- Discovery and activation ≥ 4; Instruction adherence ≥ 4; Evidence
  grounding ≥ 4.
- No criterion at 1; no critical failure.
